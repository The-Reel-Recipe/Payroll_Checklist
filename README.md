# Payroll Checklist

An interactive, offline guide to running **The Reel Recipe's** monthly payroll, written for HR.

It assumes no prior knowledge of payroll or of the Airtable system — every term is explained,
every step says which page to open, and every error message the system can produce is listed
with its fix.

## Use it

Open **[`index.html`](index.html)** in any browser (double-click). Works offline, Mac or Windows.

- **Type the month** you're paying at the top. Ticks are saved separately per month.
- **Tap a step** to open the how-to, with a screenshot of the real page.
- **Tap the circle** to tick it off. Progress saves in that browser.
- Press **Ctrl / ⌘ + P** to print or save as PDF.

## The run — 12 steps, 3 parts

**Part 1 · Get everything in and approved**
1. Chase the managers for overtime, KPIs and leave
2. Approve the bonuses, advances and penalties
3. Decide which warnings cost money
4. Mark the holidays and closures on the calendar
5. Check the salary cards, and close off anyone who left
6. Import the attendance, then look at what came in

**Part 2 · Build the payslips and clean them up**
7. Check the month says "ready", then tick the box
8. Read the card check and fix whoever it names
9. Empty the "Needs fixing" list
10. Sense-check the numbers before you commit

**Part 3 · Approve and pay — one way only**
11. Approve — the numbers freeze for good
12. Mark Paid — this is what emails everyone

## Also inside

- **What the red messages mean** — all 12 blocker messages, searchable, each with what it
  means, who fixes it, and the steps to fix it.
- **Who approves what** — the manager / HR split, in one table.
- **Words you'll see** — plain-English definitions of payslip, salary card, prorated,
  deduction day, adjustment, KPI pot, snapshot, void.
- **"Something looks wrong"** — quick answers to the six things that come up most.
